ABOUT US

Higher Days, Brighter You

HayonCampus was born from a fundamental frustration in college life: students had to switch between four different disconnected websites just to check where their next class was, whether they could afford to miss a lecture, where to find syllabus notes, and how to recruit classmates for hackathons.

We built HayonCampus as the parent ecosystem to solve both sides of college existence:

1. Physical Campus Life (CampusAssist)

Handling every real-world college constraint: class timings, room changes, 75% attendance criteria, exam seating, and campus navigation.

2. Academic Collaboration (StudyOS)

Handling every intellectual growth requirement: peer notes, lab assignments, project repositories, and interdisciplinary team building.

With a single Firebase-backed credential, HayonCampus unifies your entire collegiate footprint under one secure roof.
