ABOUT HAYONCAMPUS

Purpose-built for the rhythm of modern university life.

University life moves at breakneck speed. Students traditionally juggle disjointed spreadsheets for attendance, fragmented group chats for classroom notes, and buried emails when recruiting teammates for hackathons.

Hayoncampus was built to unify these realities into two synchronized, high-performance engines: CampAssist for day-to-day schedule and attendance survival, and StudyOS for collaborative academic excellence.
